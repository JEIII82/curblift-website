Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how RinsePoint Exterior Cleaning handles information submitted through rinsepoint.com.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, city, requested service, project details, and photos you send by text.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to quote requests, communicate about scheduling and service details, provide requested services, and maintain basic business records.

Quote-form provider

The website quote form is processed through Formspree. Information submitted through the form may be processed by Formspree so the request can be delivered to RinsePoint. Formspree's own privacy terms may also apply to its processing.

Sharing and selling information

RinsePoint does not sell customer contact information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide requested services, or when required by law.

Data retention

We keep quote and customer information only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, service records, business operations, and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you previously submitted, subject to records we may need to retain for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, call or text (972) 379-7161.
